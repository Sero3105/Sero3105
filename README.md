## `> about`

Third-year **Computer Science for Management** student at the **University of Bologna**. I'm working toward cybersecurity through the foundations that make it real: software engineering, networking, Linux, operating systems and cryptography.

I work from first principles. Instead of treating a tool as a black box, I want to understand its data, its boundaries and the trade-offs it makes. Projects are where I test that mindset — turning an idea into a usable application, then refining the details that make it clearer, safer and easier to maintain.
